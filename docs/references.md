# Suggested references

Books and articles for further study. The notebooks are self-contained and do not
follow any of them; the list is a starting point for readers who want more.

## The Onsager principle and soft matter

* M. Doi, *Soft Matter Physics* (Oxford University Press, 2013).
* M. Doi and S. F. Edwards, *The Theory of Polymer Dynamics* (Oxford University Press, 1986).
* S. R. de Groot and P. Mazur, *Non-Equilibrium Thermodynamics* (North-Holland, 1962; Dover, 1984).
* P.-G. de Gennes, F. Brochard-Wyart and D. Quéré, *Capillarity and Wetting Phenomena* (Springer, 2004).
* W. B. Russel, D. A. Saville and W. R. Schowalter, *Colloidal Dispersions* (Cambridge University Press, 1989).

## Fluid mechanics, diffusion and variational methods

* G. K. Batchelor, *An Introduction to Fluid Dynamics* (Cambridge University Press, 1967).
* J. Crank, *The Mathematics of Diffusion*, 2nd ed. (Oxford University Press, 1975).
* B. A. Finlayson, *The Method of Weighted Residuals and Variational Principles* (Academic Press, 1972; SIAM, 2014).
* I. M. Gelfand and S. V. Fomin, *Calculus of Variations* (Prentice-Hall, 1963; Dover, 2000).
* J. Happel and H. Brenner, *Low Reynolds Number Hydrodynamics* (Prentice-Hall, 1965; Martinus Nijhoff, 1983).
* H. Lamb, *Hydrodynamics*, 6th ed. (Cambridge University Press, 1932).
* L. D. Landau and E. M. Lifshitz, *Fluid Mechanics*, 2nd ed. (Pergamon, 1987).
* L. G. Leal, *Advanced Transport Phenomena* (Cambridge University Press, 2007).
